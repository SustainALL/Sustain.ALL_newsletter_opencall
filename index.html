About the hub

Education for sustainable development, with your challenge at the centre

Sustain.ALL is an educational and transformation initiative at the Technische Universität Berlin that links Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) with real-world processes of change. Instead of case studies, our students work directly on challenges brought to us by partners like you.

Challenge-based, not just conceptual

Alongside traditional coursework, teams spend the semester on one live problem — yours — from research through to a working prototype.

The whole university contributes

Administration, faculties, students and researchers all feed challenges, data and expertise into the hub — and get a working solution back.

A bridge, not a silo

We sit between society, industry and academia, encouraging the transdisciplinary collaboration that climate resilience actually needs.

Already tested — with results

We ran this model for a full semester: seven clusters, nine projects, and teams that moved from concept to pilot. See for yourself below.

For faculty & partners

What it looks like to work with us

Four steps, one semester. You stay the domain expert — we handle the pedagogy, the team, and the pace.

01

Share your challenge

Tell us what isn't working — a process, a dataset, a wasted material stream, an unused space. No polished brief required.

02

We match a team

An interdisciplinary student group takes it on inside the real-world-lab course, under the cluster it fits best.

03

You mentor, we coach

You stay involved as the domain expert on a few check-ins a semester; our teaching team runs the weekly coaching.

04

It graduates onward

Projects move from concept to prototype to pilot — and we help hand the result back into your department or org.

Bring us your challenge

Have a problem nobody's had time to solve properly?

That's exactly what this hub is for. Fill in a few lines below — we'll get back to you before the next semester's teams are matched.

  • No formal proposal needed — a rough problem description is enough to start.
  • You choose how involved you are: a few check-ins, or hands-on mentoring.
  • Micro-funding (€500–€2,500) is available through Sustain.FINANCE for pilots that need it.

We reply to every submission within two weeks — usually sooner.
