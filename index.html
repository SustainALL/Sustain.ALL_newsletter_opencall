About the hub

Education for sustainable development, with your challenge at the centre

Sustain.ALL is an educational and transformation initiative at the Technische Universität Berlin that links Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) with real-world processes of change. Instead of case studies, our students work directly on challenges brought to us by partners like you.

Execution-based Teaching

Alongside traditional coursework, teams spend the semester on real world challenges, such as the one you would propose, all the way from research through to a working prototype.

University-wide Collaboration

Administration, faculties, students and researchers all feed challenges, data and expertise into the hub to achieve a working solution, collectively.

Bridge between different worlds

We sit between society, industry and academia, encouraging the transdisciplinary collaboration that climate resilience deseres.

For Faculty & Partners

Working with us

You stay the domain expert — we handle the pedagogy, the team, and the pace.

01

Challenge Creation

Tell us what isn't working — a process, a dataset, a wasted material stream, an unused space.

02

Challenge Matching

An interdisciplinary student group undertakes the challenge under our module.

03

Mentoring & Coaching

You stay involved as the domain expert conducting check-ins during the semester while we run the weekly coaching.

04

Future Progression

Depending on complexity, projects mature to a pre-pilot or prototype. We help with implementing the prototype or onboarding a new team to continue the challenge next semester.
